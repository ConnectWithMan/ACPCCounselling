Admission Authority & System

ACPC Counselling 2026 — Overview & Official Highlights

The Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC) functions under the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Government of Gujarat, headquartered at the prestigious L.D. College of Engineering (LDCE) campus in Ahmedabad. It regulates centralized, merit-driven admissions into professional undergraduate and postgraduate courses across government, grant-in-aid, and self-financed institutions throughout Gujarat.

ACPC Counselling is the single-window gateway for candidates seeking admission to professional programs including Bachelor of Engineering / Technology (B.E./B.Tech), Pharmacy (B.Pharm/D.Pharm), Architecture (B.Arch), Planning & Interior Design (B.Plan / B.ID), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Computer Applications (MCA), and Diploma to Degree (D2D) lateral admissions.

Merit Marks Architecture

ACPC 2026 Merit Calculation & The 50:50 Formula Explained

Unlike many other states that rely solely on an entrance test, Gujarat follows an empirical composite model that combines board theoretical proficiency with competitive aptitude.

Official Gujarat Formula

ACPC B.E. / B.Tech Composite Merit Formula

Composite Merit Marks (100) = [ 0.50 × Board PCM Theory PR ] + [ 0.50 × GUJCET PR ]

PR = Percentile Rank. The Board Percentile Rank is derived strictly from the Theory marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (excluding practicals). The GUJCET Percentile Rank is determined from the combined score of Physics, Chemistry, and Maths papers (out of 120 marks).

Gujarat Board (GSEB) Candidates Percentile ranks are directly issued by the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB) and mapped with GUJCET scores.
CBSE / ICSE School Candidates in Gujarat Normalized percentile ranks are computed by ACPC using GSEB equivalence parameters based on aggregate theory PCM marks.
All India Quota (Outside Gujarat) Admissions under the 5% All India quota in Government colleges are determined 100% on the basis of All India Rank (AIR) in JEE Main 2026.

Gujarat Seat Reservation Policy

ACPC 2026 Reservation Matrix, Quotas & TFWS Scheme

Gujarat maintains an organized reservation policy across state institutions, complemented by supernumerary quotas such as TFWS for economically weaker students.

1. State Domicile Quota

95% Seats in Govt & Grant-in-Aid Colleges: Reserved exclusively for candidates who have completed their qualifying 10+2 examinations from schools located in Gujarat State.

5% All India Quota: Filled on the basis of All India Rank in JEE Main 2026 for both Gujarat and Outside-Gujarat candidates.

2. Category Reservation

  • Scheduled Caste (SC): 7% seats
  • Scheduled Tribe (ST): 15% seats
  • SEBC (OBC of Gujarat): 27% seats (Requires Parishishta-4 Non-Creamy Layer Certificate)
  • EWS (Economically Weaker Section): 10% seats (Parishishta-G)
  • Open / General Category: 41% unreserved seats

3. TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver)

5% Supernumerary Seats: Over and above the sanctioned intake in each branch in all government and private institutions.

100% Tuition Fee Exemption: Candidate pays ₹0 tuition fees throughout the entire 4-year degree course. Open to all categories whose family income is less than ₹8.00 Lakhs per annum.

4. Horizontal Quotas

  • PwD (Persons with Disabilities): 5% horizontal reservation within each category for candidates with ≥ 40% physical disability.
  • Defense Personnel / Ex-Servicemen: 1% priority reservation for children of defense personnel serving or settled in Gujarat.
  • Female Reservation: Horizontal quota for girls in designated technical branches.

Qualification Standards

ACPC Counselling 2026 — Program-Wise Eligibility Directory

Review the mandatory educational qualifications, minimum qualifying percentage, and compulsory subjects for each professional course stream.

Undergraduate Engineering

B.E. / B.Tech (4 Years)

  • Passed 10+2 (HSC) or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry / Biology / Computer Science / Biotechnology.
  • Minimum 45% marks in theory PCM (40% for SC/ST/SEBC/EWS candidates of Gujarat).
  • Must have appeared in GUJCET 2026 (for Gujarat State Quota) OR JEE Main 2026 Paper 1 (for All India Quota).
  • Appearing 12th board candidates are conditionally eligible.
Pharmacy & Allied

B.Pharm & D.Pharm (4 / 2 Years)

  • Passed 10+2 (Science Stream) with Physics and Chemistry as compulsory subjects, along with Mathematics or Biology.
  • Minimum 45% marks in qualifying subjects (40% for SC/ST/SEBC/EWS candidates).
  • Must have a valid score in GUJCET 2026 (PCM or PCB group) or qualifying NEET 2026 score as per latest ACPC rules.
Architecture & Planning

B.Arch & B.Plan (5 / 4 Years)

  • For B.Arch: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (min 50% aggregate) OR a 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics (min 50% aggregate).
  • Valid score in NATA 2026 (National Aptitude Test in Architecture) is strictly mandatory.
  • For B.Plan: 10+2 with Mathematics as a compulsory subject with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved categories).
Postgraduate Management & Tech

MBA & MCA (2 Years)

  • Recognized Bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration with at least 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST/SEBC/EWS).
  • For MCA: Candidate must have passed Mathematics / Business Mathematics / Statistics at the 10+2 level or at the Graduation level.
  • Must have appeared in CMAT 2026 (Common Management Admission Test) conducted by NTA.
Lateral Entry (Direct 2nd Year)

D2D Engineering & Pharmacy

  • Completed a 3-year AICTE-approved Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering & Technology / D.Pharm from a recognized institute.
  • Minimum 45% marks or equivalent qualifying SPI/CPI in the final year (40% for reserved categories).
  • Admission is granted directly into the 3rd semester (2nd year) of B.E./B.Tech based on DDCET / Diploma merit.

System Rules & Allotment Logic

ACPC 2026 — Counselling Rounds & Upward Movement Protocol

Understand the four distinct phases of ACPC seat allocation, how choices are processed by the algorithm, and how upward reshuffling protects your confirmed seat.

Phase 01

Mock Allotment Round

A trial simulation round designed to let candidates test choice orders. Mock allotment indicates your probable admission college without freezing any seat.

Action: Review closing ranks, add more safe options, and re-order preferences before Round 1 choice locking.
Phase 02

Round 1 Allotment

The primary centralized allocation round. If a seat is allotted, candidate must pay the online token fee (₹1,500 Govt / ₹20,000 SFI) to confirm the admission slip.

Action: Confirm seat online. If satisfied, freeze admission; if desiring a higher choice, opt for Round 2 Upward Reshuffling.
Phase 03

Round 2 (Reshuffling)

Upward movement round. Your confirmed Round 1 seat is 100% protected unless a higher preferred choice is allotted. Unallotted candidates also participate.

Action: If higher seat is allotted, earlier seat is cancelled automatically; if no higher seat is allotted, Round 1 seat remains intact.
Phase 04

Institute Spot Round

Conducted directly by individual colleges for vacant and management quota seats remaining after ACPC centralized online rounds conclude.

Action: Apply directly at college websites/campuses. Admissions are granted strictly based on merit rank among applicants.

Admission Cancellation & Refund Policy

Candidates wishing to withdraw from ACPC admissions can cancel their confirmed seat online via their candidate login within the specified cancellation deadline.

  • Upon online cancellation, the candidate's admission is voided, and the seat is transferred to the vacant pool for subsequent rounds.
  • Token tuition fees paid online (₹1,500 for Govt or ₹20,000 for SFI) are refunded to the candidate's registered bank account after deducting standard administrative processing charges per ACPC notification.
Step-by-Step Procedure

6-Stage ACPC 2026 Admission Roadmap

Follow this systematic roadmap from registration to physical reporting to ensure a frictionless admission journey.

01

Online User Registration & Profile Setup

Stage 1

Visit gujacpc.admissions.nic.in. Click on "New Candidate Registration", enter valid mobile number, email ID, and create a strong password. Complete basic personal details, address, and board credentials.

02

Document Upload & Application Fee Payment

Stage 2

Upload scanned passport-size photograph, signature, 10th & 12th marksheet, GUJCET/JEE scorecards, and category/income certificates. Pay the non-refundable registration fee of ₹350/- online.

03

Merit List Verification & Rank Card

Stage 3

ACPC verifies documents online (or via Help Centres if discrepancies arise). Check the Provisional Merit List to review your State General Rank, Category Rank, and Composite Merit Marks.

04

Mock Choice Filling & Preference Optimization

Stage 4

Fill maximum college-branch combinations in preferential order. Check the Mock Round result to evaluate your admission probability, then re-arrange choices for Round 1 and lock them before deadline.

05

Seat Allotment & Online Token Fee Payment

Stage 5

When Round 1 allotment is declared, log in to check your allotted institute and branch. To accept the seat, pay the token tuition fee (₹1,500 Govt / ₹20,000 SFI) online and download the official Admission Slip.

06

Institute Reporting & Document Verification

Stage 6

Report to the allotted college along with original documents, Admission Slip, fee payment receipt, and two sets of self-attested photocopies. Complete institutional joining formalities to commence classes.

Verification Protocols

ACPC 2026 — Documents Required for Registration & Reporting

Prepare original documents along with two sets of self-attested photocopies. Category and income certificates must strictly conform to Gujarat Government formats.

1. Academic Credentials

  • SSC (10th Standard) Marksheet: Required for verification of date of birth and candidate name.
  • HSC (12th Standard Science) Marksheet: Mandatory for PCM/PCB theory marks verification.
  • GUJCET 2026 Scorecard: Printed copy of the official GUJCET result card showing percentile rank.
  • JEE Main 2026 Score Card: Required if applying under the 5% All India Quota in Govt/GIA institutions.
  • NATA 2026 / CMAT 2026 Scorecard: Mandatory for Architecture (B.Arch) or MBA/MCA admissions.

2. Identity & Domicile

  • School Leaving Certificate (L.C.) / Transfer Certificate: Proof of birth place, date of birth, and Indian nationality.
  • Aadhaar Card: Mandatory Government photo ID for biometric verification.
  • Gujarat Domicile Certificate: Required if candidate passed 10th or 12th from outside Gujarat but parents are Gujarat domicile.
  • Passport Size Photographs: 4 to 6 recent color photographs identical to the one uploaded during registration.

3. Category & TFWS Certificates

  • Caste Certificate (SC / ST / SEBC): Issued by the designated competent authority of Gujarat State.
  • Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate: Strictly mandatory for SEBC candidates (Parishishta-4 / પરિશિષ્ટ-૪) issued after April 1 of the qualifying financial year.
  • EWS Certificate: Issued in designated Gujarat Government format (Parishishta-G / પરિશિષ્ટ-ગ).
  • Income Certificate (for TFWS): Showing annual family income < ₹8.00 Lakhs issued by Mamlatdar / TDO.
  • Disability Certificate: Issued by Civil Surgeon for PwD candidates (≥ 40% disability).
